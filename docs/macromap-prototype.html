Features

Everything the workflow needs — nothing it doesn't.

Seven features across connected data sources: recipes, nutrition, and your own preferences.

🎯

TDEE & Macro Onboarding

Mifflin-St Jeor calculation with an editable suggested macro split for cut, bulk, or maintain.

🥗

Preferences & Constraints

Dietary style, allergy flags, and dislikes — safety filtering that's always applied.

💬

Conversational Plan Assistant

Chat alongside every step — edit your pantry, swap a meal, or change a constraint in plain language instead of hunting through forms.

🍽️

Meal Plan Generation

21 macro-matched meals a week, sourced from a 5,000+ recipe library and your own pantry. When nothing fits, the assistant composes or tweaks a recipe — or adds a small side — to close the gap.

🛒

Grocery List

Deduped shopping list with consolidated quantities and one-tap copy.

🧂

Pantry Log

Log what's already at home — the plan is built around it from the start, and your grocery list never suggests buying it again.

📅

Calendar Export

One tap turns your weekly plan into a downloadable calendar file — Google, Apple, or Outlook.
