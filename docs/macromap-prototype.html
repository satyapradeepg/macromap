The problem

Four apps. One goal. Zero conversations between them.

People eating to a macro or budget target lose hours every week planning meals, checking nutrition, building grocery lists, and doing mental math to stay on budget. The tools exist — they just don't talk to each other.

Onboarding

Weight, height, age, activity level, and goal (cut / bulk / maintain) — entered once. MacroMap calculates your TDEE with the Mifflin-St Jeor formula and suggests daily macro targets. Accept them or nudge any value yourself.

Weekly Cycle

Day 7 (or whenever you're ready), MacroMap pre-fills next week from your saved profile. Any meal you rated low is automatically excluded from future plans — the app gets a little better at knowing your taste every week. Try generating next week — it loops back to Meal Plan.
